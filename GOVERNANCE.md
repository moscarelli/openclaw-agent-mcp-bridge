# Governance

## Current model

The project currently has one maintainer: `@moscarelli`. The maintainer owns
scope, security, releases, repository settings, and final merge decisions.

All paths are covered by `CODEOWNERS`. External contributions require review
from the maintainer. Automated checks are evidence, not a substitute for human
review.

## Changes

- Normal changes are proposed through pull requests.
- Security-sensitive changes require threat-model review and relevant tests.
- The default and development branches are permanent and protected.
- Topic branches should be deleted after merge.
- Experimental behavior must not be presented as stable or official.

## Growth

Additional maintainers may be appointed after sustained, trusted contribution.
Any change to ownership, approval rights, or release authority will be recorded
publicly in this document before it takes effect.
