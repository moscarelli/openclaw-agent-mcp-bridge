# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and
welcoming. Contributors must treat others with professionalism and empathy,
regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code, not people.
- Give actionable, technically grounded feedback.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, private logs, session data,
  or personal information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and release readiness.
- Report security issues through the private process in `SECURITY.md`.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption, impersonation,
doxxing, publication of private data, and sexualized or otherwise abusive
conduct are not permitted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer using GitHub's private
reporting options when available. The maintainer may edit or remove content,
close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban
participants when necessary to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, severity, repetition, and the
safety of affected participants. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical.
